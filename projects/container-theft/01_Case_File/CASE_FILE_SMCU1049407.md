## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (read first)

801, LLC purchased sleeping bags from a Chinese factory ("High Hope") on **DDP terms** with a Chinese-side shipping arranger ("Dahlen Foward Shipping Supply Chain (Shanghai) Co., Ltd."). All-in DDP shipping was paid up front: **$6,474.61** wired 3/11/26.

The shipment was filed into US customs **without a valid Power of Attorney from 801, LLC** and with the **wrong consignee** ("Greatland" / "Great Way Trading & Transportation") and the **wrong commodity description** ("fishing line"). CBP placed an enforcement hold on **2/11/26**.

The container then sat at SSA Marine Terminal 18 in Seattle from arrival on **3/16/26** until **4/21/26** — 36 days — while the chain of US-side parties (DK Group entities, Great Way / OEC Group, ICCB customs broker) scrambled to obtain a valid POA from 801, LLC (finally provided 3/29/26) and to fix the filing. Container was FULLY CLEARED on **4/7/26**.

**DK paid SSA $14,617.02 in demurrage on 4/21/26 (their own receipt).**
**DK then invoiced 801, LLC for $34,000** to release the goods — roughly **5x the agreed all-in DDP shipping cost**, and well above their own internal cost (~$26,920 per their own "Review the bill" workbook).

SSA Marine has confirmed in writing that the container **never left the terminal for inspection** despite DK repeatedly representing it was under "intensive inspection." US Customs (Seattle CBPO Butler) is **actively investigating** and has sent 801 a formal request for information on 4/29/26.

## 5. KEY FACTUAL ALLEGATIONS

### A. Unauthorized Customs Filing — Now Documented
- Container was filed with **wrong consignee on the master B/L** (Great Way Trading & Transportation, not 801, LLC)
- DK provided a **POA Andrew never executed**; original CBP filing went in under that
- **CBP held the container on 2/11/26**, then again on 2/27/26 specifically demanding "Upload Entry Documents, POA, Importer Identification"
- ICCB only obtained a valid POA from 801 on **3/29/26** — 46 days after the original hold
- Container was FULLY CLEARED on 4/7/26, ~8 business days after valid POA submission
- **Inference: the entire 2/11–4/7 delay (and resulting demurrage) is attributable to the unauthorized filing and DK's failure to obtain a valid POA up front.**

### B. Misclassified Goods (Potential Customs Fraud — 19 U.S.C. § 1592)
- Goods filed as **"fishing line" / "fishing goods"** when actual cargo is **sleeping bags**
- Different HTS code, different duty/tariff rate
- Andrew was charged the correct duty for sleeping bags — confirms the duty side was eventually corrected, but the entry summary itself appears to have remained misclassified
- Andrew cannot locate a 7501 (Entry Summary) — preservation/discovery priority

### C. Misrepresentation of Inspection Status
- DK repeatedly told Andrew container was under "intensive inspection"
- **SSA Marine (Brittney Nguyen, Tiffany) confirms in writing the container never left Terminal 18** for any inspection
- DK's own "Review the bill" workbook nonetheless includes a **$3,215 "Inspection fees" line** — being passed through to 801

### D. Demurrage Manufactured by Defendants' Own Delay
- LFD: 3/20/26 — container was free to be picked up
- DK did not retrieve until 4/21/26 — **32 days of demurrage caused by their own POA/filing failures**
- All demurrage during this window is DK's responsibility under DDP

### E. DDP Term Shipment — Already Paid in Full
- **801 paid $6,474.61 on 3/11/26 to Dahlen Foward Shipping Supply Chain (Shanghai) for all-in DDP delivery**
- Under DDP, seller and seller's agents bear all costs and risks until delivery to buyer's door
- Lucas (DK) admitted on 5/4/26 11:52 AM: **"We handled both the booking and customs clearance on your behalf"** — directly establishes agency under the DDP arrangement
