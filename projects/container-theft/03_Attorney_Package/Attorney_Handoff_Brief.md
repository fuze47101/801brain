## 1. THE 60-SECOND VERSION

801, LLC purchased sleeping bags from a Chinese factory on **DDP terms** for delivery to my customer Worldwide Distributors in **Kent, WA**. All-in DDP shipping was paid up front via wire on March 11, 2026 — **$6,474.61** to Dahlen Foward Shipping Supply Chain (Shanghai) Co., Ltd.

The shipment was filed into US Customs **without a valid POA from 801** and with the **wrong consignee** (Great Way Trading & Transportation) and the **wrong commodity classification** ("fishing line" instead of sleeping bags). CBP placed an enforcement hold. The container then sat at SSA Marine Terminal 18 in Seattle from **3/16/26 to 4/21/26** while a chain of US-side parties (DK Group entities + Great Way / OEC Group + ICCB customs broker) scrambled to get a valid POA from me — which they finally received on **3/29/26**. CBP cleared the container on **4/7/26**, eight business days later.

**DK Group then paid SSA $14,617.02 in demurrage on 4/21/26 (their own receipt)** and turned around and **invoiced me ~$34,000** to release the goods. SSA Marine has confirmed in writing that the container **never left the terminal for inspection** despite DK's repeated representations that it was under "intensive inspection."

**The container is currently being held by Portland Container** (drayage, friendly bailee). CBP Seattle (CBPO Butler) opened an active information request on 4/29/26.

I refuse to pay the inflated invoice. I want my goods released to me and I want these parties on the record for what they did, ideally in a way that protects other importers from being run through the same scheme.
