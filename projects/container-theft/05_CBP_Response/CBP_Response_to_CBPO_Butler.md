## 8. Additional context I want to make sure you have

A few items that you may find useful in the investigation, beyond your direct questions:

1. **Consignee substitution.** The master B/L was filed with **Great Way Trading & Transportation Inc** as both consignee and notify party — neither I nor 801, LLC was identified as the consignee on the entry, despite being the actual importer / product owner. (Earlier in the email thread there is also reference to "Greatland" as a separately substituted consignee at one point; I am still trying to confirm whether "Greatland" and "Great Way" are the same entity or two different substitutions.)

2. **Commodity misclassification.** The shipment was filed as **"fishing line"** (per the OEC/Great Way invoice and DK's communications). The container actually contains **sleeping bags**. I was eventually charged the correct duty for sleeping bags, but the entry classification appears to have been misdescribed.

3. **Demurrage manufactured by the agents' own delay.** Last Free Day was March 20, 2026. The container was not retrieved from SSA Terminal 18 until April 21, 2026 — 32 days later — entirely because the agents had not obtained a valid POA from me and could not get the entry cleared. SSA Marine has confirmed in writing (Brittney Nguyen, April 28) that the container never left the terminal during this period.

4. **"Inspection" representations.** I was repeatedly told by DK that the container was under "intensive inspection." SSA Marine has confirmed in writing that no such inspection occurred and the container did not leave Terminal 18.

5. **Inflated invoice.** DK paid SSA $14,617.02 in demurrage on April 21, 2026 (Purchase #C0007115444, VISA-8708, signed by Alice Zhang as DK Freight). DK then invoiced me approximately $34,000. DK's own internal cost workbook (which I have a copy of) shows their total cost at approximately $27,682. I refused to pay and the container is now being held by Portland Container in Oregon.

6. **Pattern.** I have been told by another company in the industry that DK has done this same pattern to other importers in 2026. I am respecting that source's request not to be disclosed by name, but I would point your investigators toward FMC dockets, FMCSA records on MC #1557743 / USDOT #4089849, BBB / California AG complaints, and PACER for any independent corroboration.
